Proof Beta

Proof is in active beta development and currently free to download. No checkout or email address is required.

Proof Is In Beta

Proof is usable today, but it is still being actively developed. That means parts of the workflow may change, bugs may appear, and some planned features are still being refined before they become part of the stable app experience.

The current beta is focused on fast local photo culling, folder-based library work, ratings, flags, labels, comparison views, Fuji X-Raw, local catalog data, and non-destructive Mac Develop and export. Future releases may change behavior as the product improves.

Free To Download

During beta, Proof can be downloaded for free. You do not need to pay to try it, test it with your own photo folders, or decide whether it fits your workflow.

If you are evaluating Proof for production work, keep normal backups of your photo library and catalog. Beta software should be treated with the same caution as any actively changing tool.

Download Directly

Download for Mac, open the DMG, and drag Proof into Applications. The universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 11 or later.

Help Shape Proof

Join optional emails for feature releases and occasional beta surveys. Confirm your email to join, and unsubscribe anytime. Downloading Proof or sending feedback does not subscribe you.

Platform Availability

The public download is for Mac. iPad is in development; this website does not currently offer an iPad download.

Develop is in beta. Rendering and advanced adjustment tools are still being refined. Keep backups and check your exports before using them for final delivery.

Feedback Helps

Bug reports, workflow notes, and clear reproduction steps are useful during beta development. The dedicated feedback form asks for the details that are most helpful, including your Proof and macOS versions, the workflows you tested, and any steps that reproduce an issue.
