01 / The first pass

A shoot becomes
a selection.

Start with your folders. Keep RAW and camera-rendered companions together. Flag, rate, and label as you find the photographs you want to keep.

P / X / U

Pick. Reject. Undecided.

A first pass can be that simple. Your photographs stay in the folders you choose; the catalog stays on your Mac.

02 / Make the call

Give each frame
some room.

See selected photographs together in Survey. Open Focus Mode for more space, then move into Loupe to inspect focus and detail.

Closer, when it counts

The details can change your mind.

Move from the whole photograph to the small things that make it a keeper. Flags and ratings remain close at hand.

03 / Fuji X-Raw · Camera development & recipes

Plug in your Fuji.
Make your next look.

Develop your RAF files through a connected Fujifilm camera. Explore its film simulations, build new recipes, and see the colour rendered by the camera itself, right inside Proof.

01 / CONNECT

Your camera does the developing.

Connect a supported Fujifilm camera in USB RAW-conversion mode. Select a RAF in Proof and use the camera’s own rendering to develop it.

02 / CREATE

Find a look. Make it yours.

Choose a film simulation and shape your recipe with the settings your camera supports. Render a preview, compare the result, then convert.

03 / REUSE

Keep the recipe, too.

Save your recipes for another photograph. Import and export recipe text to bring favourite looks into your workflow.

Explore camera development & recipes ↗

Requires a supported Fujifilm camera and USB RAW-conversion mode. Available settings depend on the camera and source photograph.

Develop / In beta

Develop on Mac with non-destructive adjustments. Edits stay in the catalog; export creates a new rendered file. Develop is in beta and still being refined.
